Start using Ballpark

Calibrate unfamiliar numbers as you read. Ballpark underlines figures on any page — click one to see whether it's large, small, or typical for its context, with comparisons.

  1. 1

    Add your Anthropic API key

    Ballpark is bring-your-own-key: calibrations run on your Anthropic account, so your reading never touches our servers. You only pay Anthropic for what you use (calibrations use the low-cost Haiku model — typically a fraction of a cent each).

    Don't have one? Create an API key → (sign in, then Create Key). The key starts with sk-ant-.

  2. 2

    Pin Ballpark to your toolbar

    So you can re-open these settings anytime: click the puzzle-piece icon (Extensions) at the top-right of Chrome, then click the pin next to Ballpark. The Ballpark icon now stays in your toolbar — click it to update your key.

  3. 3

    Start reading

    Open any article with numbers in it. Numbers Ballpark recognizes get a dotted underline — click one to calibrate it.
